Executive Summary

As we navigate the complex landscape of 2025, thought leadership has evolved from a marketing buzzword to a strategic business imperative. In an increasingly crowded marketplace where trust is the ultimate currency, organizations that authentically showcase their expertise and vision capture disproportionate market share and command premium positioning.

"In today's hyper-competitive talent and recruitment space, thought leadership is no longer optional—it's the price of entry for category leadership."

For Indeed, establishing a dedicated Thought Leadership & Strategic Engagement function represents a significant opportunity to:

Strengthen C-Suite Relationships

Create meaningful engagement with decision-makers through value-driven insights

Amplify Brand Authority

Position Indeed as the definitive voice on the future of work and hiring

Drive Revenue Growth

Generate attributable pipeline and accelerate deal velocity through expertise-based engagement

This proposal outlines how a strategic thought leadership function can serve as a connective tissue across Indeed's existing initiatives, amplifying their impact while creating new avenues for growth and differentiation in an increasingly AI-driven recruitment landscape.

Industry Benchmarks

Leading technology and SaaS companies have recognized the strategic value of thought leadership and have established dedicated programs to capitalize on its benefits. These benchmark organizations provide valuable insights into successful approaches and outcomes.

LinkedIn

Program Focus:

LinkedIn has built a robust thought leadership program that spans multiple channels, including the LinkedIn Business blog, LinkedIn Learning, and their B2B Institute research arm.

Key Outcomes:

  • Established the B2B Institute as the definitive voice for professional marketing insights
  • Drives 28% of enterprise leads through thought leadership content
  • Created "Advance Your Thought Leadership" learning path to position itself as the platform for leadership visibility

"LinkedIn's thought leadership program has established it as more than a platform—it's positioned the company as the definitive authority on the future of work and professional development."

Salesforce

Program Focus:

Salesforce has created a multi-faceted thought leadership approach through their Salesforce+ streaming platform, Trailblazer Community, and high-profile CEO visibility.

Key Outcomes:

  • Drives substantial upper-funnel engagement through thematic content series
  • Salesforce+ platform hosts over 1,000 expert-led videos and original series
  • Thought leadership content supports a $226B partner ecosystem

"Salesforce has masterfully used thought leadership to transform from a CRM provider to a trusted advisor on digital transformation, creating new revenue streams and partnership opportunities."

HubSpot

Program Focus:

HubSpot's thought leadership strategy centers on their blog, research reports, and educational content designed to position them as the authority on inbound marketing and sales.

Key Outcomes:

  • Generates over 7 million monthly visitors through thought leadership content
  • HubSpot Academy has certified over 200,000 professionals
  • Uses dedicated "Thought Leadership Committee" to align content with business goals

"HubSpot's thoughtful approach to content marketing and thought leadership has transformed it from a small startup to a $1.65B company that defines an entire category of marketing."

Executive Communications initiatives would gain strategic focus and expanded reach through:

  • Development of executive visibility plans aligned with business priorities
  • Content repurposing across channels to maximize value

  • Year-round content strategy to maintain engagement between events
  • Content capture and distribution to expand reach beyond attendees
  • Thought leadership programming that positions Indeed as the definitive voice on work trends

Global Brand Initiatives

Indeed's 20th Anniversary and "The World Can Work Better" global campaign would be amplified through:
